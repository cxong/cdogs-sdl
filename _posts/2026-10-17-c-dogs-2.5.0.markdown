It's time for another C-Dogs SDL release! While no major features are included, there's quite a big list of enhancements so do check it out!

The most impactful would be **DOS Pixel Aspect Ratio**. Yes, C-Dogs is a DOS game with non-square pixels, so all these years C-Dogs SDL has been [showing it wrong](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/business/no-ms-dos-games-weren-t-widescreen-tips-on-correcting-aspect-ratio)! But not anymore, and now our pot-bellied heroes regain a bit of dignity with their chiselled-jaws-look restored:
